# Stash Radar gallery

In-game shots are real screenshots taken automatically in CI (Superflat, noon, clear, 1920x1080). Title card and explainers are drawn graphics around real screenshots.

- **Stash Radar** (mod-00-title.png): Where did I put my diamonds? Search nearby containers for any item.
- **How it works** (mod-11-how-it-works.png): Press K, pick an item, and the containers that hold it light up.
- **Find it in your storage** (mod-01-storage-wall.png): /find diamond outlines the 4 containers on this wall that hold diamonds.
- **Outlines up close** (mod-02-closeup.png): Only the chest and shulker box with diamonds get an outline; their neighbours stay plain.
- **Looks inside shulker box items** (mod-12-inside-shulker.png): A chest lights up when the emeralds are inside a shulker box stored in it.
- **Shulker box item found** (mod-03-inside-shulker-item.png): Real shot: /find emerald finds the chest that holds a shulker box full of emeralds.
- **Pick how far it looks** (mod-13-range.png): The same search with range 8 finds 2 containers, with range 24 it finds all 5.
- **Search the whole yard** (mod-05-yard-range-24.png): /find iron_ingot 24 outlines every barrel, chest and shulker box with iron around the field.
- **The search screen** (mod-06-search-screen.png): Press K and type: suggestions with icons, Enter or a click searches.
- **Range button** (mod-07-search-shulker.png): Cycle the range from 8 to 64 blocks (capped by the server's max range).
- **The answer in chat** (mod-08-chat-result.png): How many you have, in how many containers, and where the nearest one is.
- **/find with tab-complete** (mod-09-tab-complete.png): Works without the mod on your game too: type /find and press Tab.
- **Checked by the server** (mod-14-server-checked.png): The server reads the real containers; your game never sends their contents.
- **Nothing nearby** (mod-10-chat-nothing.png): A clear answer when no container in range holds the item.
